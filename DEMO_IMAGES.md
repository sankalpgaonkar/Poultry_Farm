stores and farms. They do not show the real farms or products named in the demo data.

## 3D models

The landing-page 3D scene (barn, coop, hens, eggs, trees, field rows, silo) is original geometry built in code
with three.js primitives in `frontend/src/components/landing/FarmScene.jsx`. No external 3D models,
textures or HDR files are used, so there is nothing to credit.
